# 🛒 Kade Manager — Inventory & Invoice Tool for a Small Sri Lankan Kade

**Kade Manager** is a purpose-built, single-tenant **Inventory Management and Digital Point-of-Sale (POS) web application** designed to digitize and streamline the daily operations of small retail grocery stores (*kades*) across Sri Lanka.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Small retail grocers across Sri Lanka, such as **Perera Stores**, have traditionally relied on physical paper notebooks, locally known as the **"Potha"**, to manage their daily business operations.

While this manual approach is simple and familiar, it can lead to several operational challenges, including:

* Difficulty tracking inventory accurately
* Delays in identifying low-stock products
* Manual and time-consuming invoice preparation
* Limited visibility into daily business performance
* Risk of errors and loss of paper-based records
* Difficulty managing access to business information

**Kade Manager** addresses these challenges by providing a digital platform for inventory, invoicing, reporting, and day-to-day store management.
